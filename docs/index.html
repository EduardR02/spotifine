Spotifine

Spotify for Windows and macOS, without the bloat.

Light enough to leave open all day, and it does what the official app won't: true random shuffle, playback speed, and cutting or looping parts of a song.

This looks like an Intel Mac. Spotifine runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) with macOS 14 or newer.

Spotifine is for Windows PCs and Apple Silicon Macs. Open this page on one of those to download it.

The first launch

Spotifine isn't signed with a paid developer certificate, so your computer asks once whether you trust it.

WindowsThis computer

Spotifine-windows-x64-setup.exe
  1. Run the installer you downloaded.
  2. If a blue box says Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Spotifine opens the Spotify login in your browser. Sign in, and you're listening.

MacThis computer

Spotifine-macos-arm64.zip
  1. Unzip the download, move Spotifine to Applications and open it.
  2. If macOS won't open it, close the message and go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down, choose Open Anyway and confirm. Then sign in to Spotify in your browser.
Extras

What the official app won't do

On top of the essentials: playlists, albums, artist pages, search, the queue, credits, radio and podcasts. No home feed, no AI DJ, no merch.

#Title
  1. Random shuffleEvery song gets the same chance, the first one too.
  2. 2 Skip songs without removing themKeep a track in a playlist but leave it out of normal playback.
  3. 3 Playback speedAnywhere from 0.5× to 4×.
  4. 4 Cut or loop part of a songCut out a section or loop an exact range, edited on a waveform, set per playlist.
  5. 5 Spotify links open herePaste a shared link into search and it opens in the app, not the web player.
  6. 6 Whole discographiesPlay everything an artist released, continuously.

Also: 320 kbps and gapless, media keys and lock screen controls, listening history kept on your computer, and updates from Settings.
